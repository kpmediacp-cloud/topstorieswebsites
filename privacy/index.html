Institutional Privacy Policy &
Confidentiality Governance Framework

TopStoriesWebsites.com ("we," "our," or "the Platform") operates a private digital mergers and acquisitions marketplace specializing in high-value online publishing properties. This Privacy Policy details the rigorous protocols we employ to govern the collection, processing, storage, and bilateral non-disclosure of personal, corporate, and proprietary financial data provided by prospective buyers, sellers, brokers, and enterprise partners.

1. Information We Collect Across Brokerage Operations

In the execution of digital asset brokerage and algorithmic due diligence, we collect several categories of information:

  • Institutional Identification Data: Full legal name, corporate entity name, registered office address, executive title, business email address, phone numbers, and official government-issued identification required for escrow anti-money laundering (AML) and Know Your Customer (KYC) compliance.
  • Proprietary Financial & Performance Metrics: Profit and loss statements, merchant gateway payouts, Google AdSense/Mediavine/Raptive earning statements, operational expense reports, bank statements, and tax documentation submitted for asset valuation.
  • Technical Digital Infrastructure Records: Google Search Console search performance data, Google Publisher Center publication configurations, domain registrar WHOIS data, server access logs, and content management system database backups.

2. Confidentiality & Bilateral Non-Disclosure Governance

We recognize that the unauthorized disclosure of a media property's identity can cause irreparable harm to its ongoing commercial operations, advertiser relationships, and search engine visibility. Consequently:

  • All listed domain names are publicly masked (e.g., h***n***s.com) across public marketplace indexes.
  • Unmasked domain dossiers, analytics screenshots, and financial records are released strictly to buyers who have executed a binding Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and demonstrated financial capacity.
  • We never sell, lease, or monetize client email lists, investor preferences, or proprietary publisher performance data to external advertising networks or data brokers.

3. Data Security, Retention & Escrow Processing

All confidential information submitted through our contact desks, NDA inquiry forms, and valuation tools is encrypted in transit via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) and stored within enterprise-grade encrypted databases with strict role-based access controls. Financial transaction processing is administered directly through our licensed partner Escrow.com, ensuring full compliance with SOC 2 Type II and California DFPI data governance standards.
