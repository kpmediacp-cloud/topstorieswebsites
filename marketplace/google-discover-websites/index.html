1. The Mechanics of the Discover Recommendation Engine: Vectors & Entity Affinity

Google Discover does not use keywords to match users with articles. Instead, it utilizes high-dimensional semantic entity embeddings generated by Google's DeepMind machine learning models (including MUM and Gemini).

When an article is crawled by Googlebot, the system extracts the primary entities (people, organizations, locations, concepts) and maps them to Google's vast Knowledge Graph. The algorithm then cross-references this entity vector against the user's personal Topic Affinity Profile, derived from their recent search history, Chrome browsing patterns, YouTube watch histories, and location signals.

When a newly published article matches an active interest vector, Google initiates an Exploration Cohort Test, displaying the card to a seed sample of 500 to 2,000 highly engaged users. If the cohort exhibits a Click-Through Rate (CTR) above 9% and an average content dwell time exceeding 45 seconds, the recommendation algorithm triggers an exponential distribution wave, pushing the article to hundreds of thousands of users across the country within 12 to 36 hours.

2. The 4 Technical Prerequisites for Sustained Discover Distribution

Google enforces explicit technical constraints before an article can enter the Discover candidate pool:

A. High-Resolution Visuals (`max-image-preview:large`)

Lead article imagery must be at least 1,200 pixels wide and served with the robots meta tag `max-image-preview:large`. Cards with high-resolution 16:9 imagery receive 330% more clicks and 4.2x greater impression allocations than standard thumbnail snippets.

B. Flawless NewsArticle & Author Schema

Discover algorithms heavily favor content with explicit E-E-A-T signals. Structured JSON-LD declaring verified author entities, organization publishing details, and clear publication dates is critical for establishing algorithmic trust.

C. Core Web Vitals Mobile Optimization

Because Discover is a 100% mobile-driven platform, any mobile performance friction terminates feed expansion. Passing Largest Contentful Paint (LCP < 2.2s) and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS < 0.05) is mandatory to sustain high algorithmic velocity.

D. Domain Interest Entity Continuity

Discover evaluates the domain's historical topical cluster focus. A publication with established authority in automotive, personal finance, or travel triggers Discover waves dramatically faster than a generalist site with fragmented topical signals.

9. Image Architecture & CDN Optimization: The Visual Engine of Discover

Lead article photography is the single greatest determining factor in Google Discover impression velocity. Google’s computer vision models evaluate visual assets before rendering cards in user feeds:

  • Resolution & Geometry: Minimum 1200px width with an exact 16:9 aspect ratio (e.g. 1200x675px or 1920x1080px). Images smaller than 1200px are automatically downgraded to low-CTR square thumbnails or excluded entirely.
  • Next-Gen Compression (WebP / AVIF): Serve images in modern formats under 150KB to ensure sub-second rendering. High-latency image loading triggers user bounce signals that suppress feed visibility.
  • Robots Directive Declaration: Every page must feature <meta name="robots" content="max-image-preview:large"> in the HTML header. Without this tag, Googlebot is legally restricted from rendering wide carousel cards in user feeds.
  • Text-Overlay Prohibition: Avoid artificial text graphics, promotional badges, or clickbait banners burned into the image. Google’s Vision API flags artificial text overlays as spam, sharply reducing feed distribution.
  • Color Contrast & Palette Saturation: Thumbnails with vibrant, balanced color palettes (high color contrast ratios) achieve an average 18% higher CTR than desaturated or monochrome graphics. High-dynamic-range imagery stands out immediately in dark-mode mobile Chrome interfaces.
  • Cross-Platform Safe Zones: Ensure primary subject matter sits within the central 60% of the image container. Both iOS Chrome and Android Discover apply slight adaptive cropping based on handset aspect ratios; central subject placement prevents awkward face or product cropping.
  • Open Graph & Twitter Card Parity: Declare identical high-resolution image paths inside `og:image` and `twitter:image` metadata tags to ensure consistent preview rendering across all mobile social sharing channels.
