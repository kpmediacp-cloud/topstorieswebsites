4. Technical Ingestion Specifications: MRSS & XML Feed Architecture

Maintaining uninterrupted syndication with Yahoo requires strict technical compliance with their ingest standards:

  1. Media RSS Namespace Integration: Feeds must declare standard XML namespaces including xmlns:media="http://search.yahoo.com/mrss/" and xmlns:dc="http://purl.org/dc/elements/1.1/".
  2. Full HTML Content Delivery: Articles must be delivered in complete, un-truncated HTML within the <content:encoded> element, including inline subheadings, blockquotes, and high-resolution images.
  3. High-Resolution Photography Enclosure: Every story must contain at least one primary image enclosed with <media:content medium="image"> with dimensions of at least 1200x675 pixels and valid copyright attribution tags.
  4. Automated Webhook Pinging: When a new story is published, your server issues an automated ping to Yahoo's partner ingest webhook, ensuring the story is scraped and live on Yahoo within 3 minutes.

8. Canonical & Duplicate Content Governance on Yahoo Syndication

A major consideration for media investors is ensuring that syndicated copies on high-authority platforms like Yahoo do not cannibalize organic rankings on the parent domain. Our assets implement a dual-governance technical protocol:

  • Cross-Domain Rel="Canonical" Tagging: Yahoo’s ingest parser is configured to accept cross-domain canonical headers pointing back to the original URL on your primary domain. Google's search algorithms recognize your site as the authoritative content originator.
  • Delayed Feed Polling Window: Articles are indexed on your primary domain via WebSub and Googlebot-News 15 to 30 minutes before the RSS feed endpoint exposes the content to Yahoo's scrapers, establishing clear chronological precedence in Google's indexing records.
  • Original Attribution Modules: Every syndicated story includes an explicit end-of-article credit box with an anchor link: "Originally published at [BrandName]. Read the full technical report [Link]." This passes high-authority referral traffic and editorial link equity back to your core property.
