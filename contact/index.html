Institutional Engagement Protocol & Confidential Due Diligence

At TopStoriesWebsites.com, we understand that transactions involving premium digital publishing assets demand rigorous confidentiality, swift execution, and institutional legal security. Whether you are an institutional fund acquiring a multi-asset news portfolio, a private family office diversifying into digital yield assets, or a veteran publisher preparing for a multi-million-dollar exit, our senior brokerage partners provide end-to-end transaction advisory.

The Four-Stage Transaction Lifecycle

Stage 1: Confidential Intake & NDA Execution

All conversations begin under a mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement. Sellers provide read-only access to Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and ad networks (Mediavine, Raptive, AdSense). Buyers submit proof of funds or corporate accreditation.

Stage 2: 48-Point Algorithmic Due Diligence

Our technical engineering team audits raw server access logs, WebSub crawl speed, Google Publisher Center ownership standing, and Moz/Ahrefs backlink topology. We generate an independent Valuation & Risk Dossier for both parties.

Stage 3: Standard Asset Purchase Agreement (APA)

Parties execute our institutional legal contract defining purchase consideration, asset warranties, non-compete clauses, and escrow terms. The buyer funds 100% of purchase consideration into licensed Escrow.com custody.

Stage 4: Technical Migration & Inspection Sign-Off

Domain EPP authorization codes, SQL databases, and Google Publisher Center Primary Ownership are transferred. The buyer conducts live test publishing during the 7-day inspection window before escrow funds are released.

Institutional Seller Guidelines & Disqualification Triggers

To protect our network of qualified buyers, our brokerage desk automatically declines to list properties exhibiting any of the following technical red flags:

  • Artificial Traffic Manipulation: Websites utilizing traffic bots, paid pop-under redirect traffic, or non-consensual browser extension injections.
  • Suppressed News Crawl Latency: Domains where newly published news sitemap URLs take longer than 5 minutes to appear in Google News search results.
  • Toxic Link Farm Footprints: Root domains with backlink profiles dominated by automated comment spam, private blog networks (PBNs), or hacked link injections.
  • Unresolved Copyright or DMCA Infringements: Domains with active intellectual property infringement lawsuits or chronic DMCA takedown notices without counter-claim resolution.
  • Publisher Center Disconnects: Sites lacking primary administrative access inside Google Publisher Center or possessing restricted publication flags.
